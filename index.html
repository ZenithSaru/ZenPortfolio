Research interests

Profile & motivation

For my final-year thesis, I used Random Forest and XGBoost models with satellite-derived land cover from Google Earth Engine to predict how forest and snow cover in Nepal may change under different climate scenarios, and to identify the most vulnerable hotspots. A second study compared hydropower projects in the Naumure, Trishuli and Arun basins under changing climate conditions.

Both projects showed me how much remote sensing and machine learning can reveal about Himalayan catchments. Alongside this research I work as a structural engineer in Kathmandu, and I hope to pursue graduate research that brings data-driven hydrological analysis together with practical engineering.

Current focusGaining field experience as a structural engineer and learning common construction practices, while two manuscripts are under review.

Research & selected projects

Academic and professional work
Undergraduate thesis · 2026 · Thapathali Campus

Forest and Snow Cover Dynamics in Nepal: Predictive Modelling and Hotspot Detection Using Machine Learning

A Random Forest and XGBoost ensemble to predict Nepal's forest and snow cover under different climate scenarios, and to detect environmental hotspots.

Role
Lead researcher, supervised by Asst. Prof. Dr. Nirajan Devkota
Methods
Random Forest LULC classification in Google Earth Engine; predictive modelling of snow and forest cover with an RF and XGBoost ensemble; vulnerability indices for hotspot detection in QGIS and Python; recommended remedies
Outcome
Developed into a research paper, now under review at the Journal of Mountain Science (Q2)
Research paper · 2026 · Nepal

Sustainability of Hydropower Projects in Nepal across Three Basins under Different Climatic Scenarios

A systematic comparison of hydropower projects in the Naumure, Trishuli and Arun basins, and how changing climate affects their power generation.

Role
Compiled the water-engineering final-year project reports and carried out the comparison
Methods
Comparison of Q40 power projections across basins under different climate scenarios
Outcome
Manuscript under review at the Journal of Innovations in Engineering Education
